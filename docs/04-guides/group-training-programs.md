# Group Training Programs: Guide for Coaches

Welcome! This guide outlines how to create, manage, and distribute training plans to groups of athletes using our "Virtual Athlete" system.

Instead of copying and pasting workouts for 20 different athletes, you simply create one **Group Program** (which acts like a virtual athlete), build the calendar there, and let the system automatically sync everything to the actual athletes' calendars.

## 1. Creating a Group Program

1. Open your Coach CRM and navigate to the **Group Programs** tab.
2. Click **Create Program**.
3. Give it a name (e.g., "UVic Tri Club - Fall Base") and an optional description.
4. Click **Create**. The system will generate a "Virtual Athlete" to hold the master calendar.

## 2. Managing the Calendar

1. On the Group Programs tab, find your newly created program and click **Manage Calendar & Plan**.
2. You will now be "acting as" the program account. Your calendar view will switch to the program's master calendar.
3. Build the training plan just like you would for a normal athlete: drag and drop workouts, apply templates, and set the schedule.
4. **Collaboration:** If another coach needs to edit the master calendar, simply invite them to coach this program (the same way you add an assistant coach to a normal athlete). They will then be able to act as the program and edit the calendar as well.

## 3. Subscribing Athletes

1. Return to your CRM's **Group Programs** tab.
2. Click **Copy Subscribe Link** under the program you want to share.
3. Send this link to your athletes (via email, Discord, Slack, etc.).
4. When an athlete clicks the link and clicks **Subscribe**, the system will immediately backfill all of the program's upcoming workouts onto their personal calendar.

## 4. Automatic Synchronization (Fan-Out)

Once an athlete is subscribed, their calendar is officially linked to the master program.

- **When you add, update, or delete** a workout on the master program's calendar, the system automatically runs a background task to instantly update the calendars of every subscribed athlete.
- **Athlete Autonomy:** The workouts are copied directly to the athlete's calendar as native workouts. This means athletes can still use the **Daily Check-in** to modify their individual version of the workout (e.g., scaling it down if they are fatigued) without affecting the master calendar or anyone else in the group.

## 5. Returning to Your Account

When you are done editing the master calendar, simply click the **"Stop Acting As"** banner at the top of your screen to return to your normal coaching view.
